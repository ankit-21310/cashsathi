# Agency and consultancy validation kit

## First conversation

1. Do you currently have unpaid client invoices?
2. How do you track due dates and follow-ups today?
3. How much time do you spend on receivables each week?
4. Which customer situations should always require your approval?
5. Would you trust low-risk reminders to send automatically after a cooldown?
6. How many active invoices do you typically manage?
7. What evidence would you need before connecting your mailbox?
8. What would make this worth paying for each month?

Do not request an invoice until the prospect understands the data boundary. Use synthetic/demo invoices in the first demonstration.

## Initial outreach copy

Subject: A safer way to follow up overdue client invoices

> Hi {{first_name}}, I’m testing a focused receivables tool for small agencies and consultancies. It reads an invoice, tracks the due date, proposes a relationship-safe follow-up, and keeps an audit trail while routing sensitive cases back to the owner. I’m looking for a 15-minute conversation about how you handle overdue invoices today. No invoice or mailbox access is needed for the interview. Would next week work?

## Evidence-safe feedback log

Record prospect ID, segment, date, interviewer, current workflow, top pain, trust boundary, willingness-to-pay signal, follow-up date, and consent status. Do not store private invoice or customer details in the interview log.

## Outreach rule

The prospect tracker contains public business channels for manual verification. No message is sent automatically, and no personal contact data should be enriched or scraped without a lawful basis.
