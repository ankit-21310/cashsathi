# Product foundations

Status: Phase 0 baseline, 14 August 2026

## Working identity

`CashSathi` is an internal codename. Public builds use the configurable name **Receivables Operator Preview** until a qualified trademark search and domain review clear a permanent name.

An initial search found an existing Indian “Cash Sathi” finance app, an active `cashsathi.com`, and a similarly named CashSarthi lending business. Search results are not legal clearance; do not launch ads, accept public payment, or file competition materials under this name without review.

## Customer and problem

The beachhead customer is an Indian B2B agency or consultancy with 3–30 active customers and no dedicated accounts-receivable team. The owner currently tracks due dates and follow-ups across invoices, spreadsheets, inboxes, and memory.

Primary jobs:

1. Know which invoices need attention today.
2. Select a relationship-safe next action from due date, history, and customer context.
3. Execute repetitive reminders consistently while escalating exceptions.
4. Prove what happened and what outcome followed.

## MVP promise

Upload an invoice. The product extracts its terms, tracks its due date, proposes the next safe collection action, executes an allowed or approved action, and maintains an auditable trail until payment is confirmed or a human must intervene.

North-star outcome: verified cash recovered or accelerated after product actions. Timing correlation is reported separately from causal attribution.

## Category positioning

Primary category: **Money & Financial Access**. Reliable conversion of completed work and invoices into cash improves a small business’s operating liquidity without CashSathi making loans or credit decisions.

## Included in the production MVP

- PDF invoice extraction with user confirmation.
- Deterministic invoice states and constrained Gemini decisions.
- Policy validation and one Gmail reminder tool.
- Approval queue, scheduler, payment confirmation, and append-oriented evidence.
- Dashboard and invoice/action timeline.

## Explicit exclusions

- Lending, credit scoring, or legal determinations.
- Threatening or coercive collection language.
- Automated legal filing, voice calls, native mobile, ERP/GST suite, or complex multi-agent orchestration.
- Closing an invoice from an unverified model inference.

## Success criteria

The product succeeds when a genuine invoice becomes validated structured data, receives a constrained and explainable AI decision, triggers a permitted real-world action, and produces a verifiable outcome record without crossing tenant or policy boundaries.
